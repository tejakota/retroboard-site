Input Method Editor RetroBoard

Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 August 2026

RetroBoard is an on-screen keyboard for Android. Because a keyboard technically sits between you and everything you type, this policy is written to state plainly what the app does and does not do with your information — and how you can verify it yourself.

Summary

RetroBoard does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no server it could send anything to.

What RetroBoard does not collect

RetroBoard does not collect, log, record, retain, or transmit:

  • anything you type, including text, passwords, or messages
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, files, or accounts
  • your location
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics

How your typing is handled

As an Android input method, RetroBoard receives the keys you tap in order to insert the corresponding characters into the app you're using. This happens entirely in memory on your device, for the moment it takes to deliver the character. Nothing you type is written to storage, kept in a history, used for learning or prediction, or sent anywhere.

RetroBoard has no word prediction, no autocorrect dictionary, no typing history, and no cloud sync — there is nothing to store and nothing to upload.

Permissions

RetroBoard requests a single Android permission: VIBRATE, used only to produce the optional haptic tick when you press a key. It grants no access to any information, and you can turn the vibration off in the app's settings.

In particular, RetroBoard does not request the INTERNET permission. Without it, the app is technically incapable of making a network connection — this isn't a promise about our conduct, it's a property of the app that anyone can verify by inspecting its manifest.

Data stored on your device

RetroBoard saves exactly three preferences locally, using Android's standard private app storage:

  • your chosen key shape (Classic or Rounded)
  • your chosen deck style (Solid or Frosted)
  • your chosen keypress feedback (sound, vibration, both, or silent)

These never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third parties

RetroBoard contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party tracking SDKs. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

RetroBoard is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL and the "Last updated" date revised. Any future version that introduces data collection would be accompanied by an app update and a corresponding change to the Data safety disclosure on Google Play.
